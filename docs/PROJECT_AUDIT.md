## B. Current architecture

`Main` is a `Control` with a container-based dashboard and inline `StyleBoxFlat` resources. It owns the presentation and forwards the night button to `GameState`. `GameState` owns the day and simulation status. `HydroponicsSimulator` listens for crop selection and night start, reads the selected `CropData`, and spends reserves through `ResourceManager`. I checked that all dashboard node paths resolve in the scene and that the serialized external resource UIDs match their files. [Scene resources and root](../scenes/main.tscn#L1) · [UI bindings](../scripts/main.gd#L3) · [Gameplay node wiring](../scenes/main.tscn#L419)

| Script | State and declarations | Methods and signals |
|---|---|---|
| `CropData` (`Resource`) | Identity fields `crop_id`, `display_name_zh`, `display_name_en`, `description`. Production fields `base_growth_requirement`, `water_per_night_ml`, `minerals_per_night_ml`, `base_harvest_quantity`, `base_market_value`. Other fields `pollution_sensitivity`, `genetic_traits`, `genetic_complexity`, `genetic_heat`, `can_produce_seeds`, `special_product_id`. No signals declared. [Dashboard controller](../scripts/main.gd#L3) |

On scene startup, `GameState` selects lettuce, and the simulator initializes its growth to zero. The dashboard reads most initial values directly because the child nodes can emit before the dashboard connects. On a click, `start_night()` sets `PROCESSING` and emits the start signal. The simulator handles it synchronously, checks water before minerals, and either reports a shortage or spends both costs and emits updated growth. `complete_night()` increments the day, emits completion and day change while status is still `PROCESSING`, then returns to `READY`. A blocked crop still advances the day. `night_processing_finished` carries day numbers only. ## D. Existing formulas

| Executed rule | Formula and source |
|---|---|
| Eligibility and debit | For each resource, `cost >= 0` and `balance >= cost`. Both checks occur before either debit. A successful debit applies `balance = max(0, balance - cost)`. Credits use the same zero floor with a positive delta. [Night checks](../scripts/hydroponics_simulator.gd#L42) · [Resource arithmetic](../scripts/resource_manager.gd#L38) |
| Growth and time | Selection resets growth to `0`. An eligible night adds `1` to growth. A completed night adds `1` to the day. [Growth](../scripts/hydroponics_simulator.gd#L23) · [Day](../scripts/game_state.gd#L49) |
| Display precision | Approximately integral amounts display as rounded integers. Other amounts display after `snappedf(value, 0.01)`. The underlying balances are floats. [Amount formatter](../scripts/main.gd#L40) |

The repository also records these **design formulas with no runtime caller**. The descriptions below reflect the repository’s research notes.

| Documented rule | Formula and source |
|---|---|
| Tablet and mineral concentration | A tablet contributes `15 ml`. Concentration is `100 × mineral_ml / pure_water_ml`, expressed as a percentage. [Formulas](FORMULAS.md#L31) |
| Mineral loss | Uptake is `actual_growth_points × uptake_per_point`. The listed rates for Nutrition Fruit, Kotton, and Coffee Beans are `0.050`, `0.075`, and `0.030 ml/point`. Total loss adds proportional mineral carry-off from the liquid draw. Exact µL allocation and rounding remain open in the notes. [Uptake](FORMULAS.md#L68) · [Liquid loss](FORMULAS.md#L215) |
| Health and mineral damage | `H₀ = 10000`, score percentage `= H / 100`. Per growth point, mineral damage is `0` in range, `50` above, and `100` below. [Health formulas](FORMULAS.md#L99) |
| Pollution damage | `Cₚ = 10000 × pollutant_volume / water_volume`, using consistent µL units. Tier input is `Cₚ × sensitivity`. The lookup loss is multiplied by the documented resistance factor, then summed over pollutants. The notes distinguish that factor from sensitivity. [Pollution formula and tier table](FORMULAS.md#L144) |
| Harvest and modifiers | Health can affect quantity or size depending on the crop. The full harvest tables, equipment formulas, and trait multipliers remain open in the research notes. [Harvest research](FORMULAS.md#L236) · [Open research](RESEARCH_NOTES.md#L241) |

### Conditional risks and open calculation decisions

- **Settlement can partially apply once a listener mutates state.** Water and minerals are debited separately, each debit emits a signal, and the simulator ignores both return values. A listener can change mineral availability after the water debit, allowing growth even if the mineral debit then fails. The current dashboard listener only updates labels. Zero-cost debits already emit redundant reserve updates twice per night. [Night debit sequence](../scripts/hydroponics_simulator.gd#L42) · [Debit signal](../scripts/resource_manager.gd#L45)
- **Settlement has multiple entry points and weak completion semantics.** A direct `process_crop_night()` call can spend resources and grow without advancing time. Missing simulator wiring can leave `GameState` in `PROCESSING`, while a missing resource manager logs a warning and still advances the day. If a callback pauses during settlement, `complete_night()` rejects the transition and `resume_simulation()` only sets `PROCESSING` again. Future listeners also need to account for completion signals firing before `READY`. [Simulator entry points](../scripts/hydroponics_simulator.gd#L14) · [Transition guards](../scripts/game_state.gd#L35)
- **Public data can bypass validation.** Reserve balances and `genetic_traits` are directly writable. An unknown resource type falls back to zero on reads, while `add_resource()` can report success without applying a change. Non-finite amounts have no explicit guard. Strict float equality at affordability boundaries can also matter once fractional mineral uptake is introduced. Direct trait writes bypass the opposing-pair and level rules. `GameState.selected_crop` and `HydroponicsSimulator.current_crop` can diverge if callers assign either field directly. [Resource API](../scripts/resource_manager.gd#L26) · [Trait API](../scripts/crop_data.gd#L52) · [Crop selection](../scripts/game_state.gd#L82)
- **Growth points and units need a contract before implementing the research formulas.** The GDD reports maturity in nights. Uptake and mineral damage use growth points. The current simulator adds one point per night and uses a fixed mineral cost. The concentration formulas need a zero-water rule, the pollution tiers need fractional boundary handling, and the liquid model needs an evaluation order and rounding policy. These are open design decisions for future calculations. [Crop baselines](FORMULAS.md#L19) · [Mineral damage](FORMULAS.md#L99) · [Pollution tiers](FORMULAS.md#L160) · [Rounding notes](FORMULAS.md#L215)

## G. Recommended next development milestone

**Make one crop playable from planting through harvest using the existing dashboard.** Set the committed entry scene, choose one explicitly configured crop, and make one night produce a consistent settlement result with resource deltas, growth, health, and any blocker. Wire a minimal planting and replenishment flow, then connect maturity to a one-time harvest result. Include basic power gating and a documented mineral concentration and uptake baseline. This gives the reviewer a complete loop and a foundation for the other two GDD crops. [Required prototype scope](GDD.md#L692)

Acceptance should cover a clean launch, exact successful-night deltas, water and power shortages with visible explanations, replenishment and recovery, concentration boundaries, maturity at the configured threshold, and a harvest that cannot pay out twice. Use the same scene path the reviewer will use for at least one integration test. [GDD acceptance cases](GDD.md#L734)
