# Visual Development Plan

## Current visual direction

The orbital workshop is the normal visual and interaction entry point. Its presentation follows an icon-based, readable control hierarchy:

- A central cultivation chamber.
- A left inventory grid.
- A bottom supply tray.
- Persistent output and collection states.
- Upper-right analysis and service tools.
- High-contrast bilingual status feedback.

Crop art, inventory icons and the workshop environment use the approved project artwork. The visual implementation preserves the final chamber composition and first-run readability established during workshop development.

## Presentation principles

- Keep hover, selection and keyboard focus visually distinct.
- Preserve mouse-first drag-and-drop interactions.
- Keep status overlays pointer-transparent.
- Keep persistent machine blockers readable.
- Preserve temporary feedback without obscuring slots or tools.
- Support 1280 × 720 and 1920 × 1080 layouts.
- Preserve Reduced Motion behavior.

## Completed visual scope

- Shared orbital theme and bilingual controls.
- Icon inventory and visual supply tray.
- Crop-specific chamber presentation.
- Machine, inventory, output and environment states.
- Farming Manual and tool tabs.
- Seed Analysis, Liquid Analysis, Equipment and DNA interfaces.
- Workshop status and night-feedback treatment.
- Sixteen procedural workshop one-shot effects.
